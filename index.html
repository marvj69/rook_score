About Rook Score

Rook Score

Fast, friendly scorekeeping for your Rook table.

Version 2.5 By Mark Heinonen

Quick Start

  1. 1 Tap a teamTap "Us" or "Dem" to begin a round.
  2. 2 Choose the bidPick the bid amount and select who took points.
  3. 3 Enter the scoreTap the score field to slide up the in-app keypad. The Us and Dem score cards update before you submit.

Pro Mode adds live win probability during play. Turn it on in Settings.

Key Features

  • Fast, Intuitive Scoring
  • Round-by-Round History
  • Save and Resume Games
  • Cloud Sync
  • Team Stats
  • Custom Themes
  • Dark Mode
  • Live Win Probability
  • Table-Talk Penalty: Flag the team that engaged in table-talk (cheating) for the round. Configure the penalty in Settings — deduct either their bid amount or a fixed number of points.

What's New in v2.5

  • Home ScreenWhen no game is in progress, Home offers Start New Game, your latest frozen game, the last result, and quick tiles for Games, Stats, Paper Game, and Settings. Open it mid-game from the menu.
  • First-Run IntroNew players get a short three-card walkthrough the first time they open the app. Replay it anytime from “How it works” on Home.
  • Full Paper Game ImportThe experimental photo import now brings in every round on the score sheet, bids included, instead of only the current score.
  • Quieter SavesRounds, settings, and presets save silently. A warning appears only when something needs attention, like full storage or a blocked microphone.
  • More Reliable SyncCleared games no longer come back after a cloud restore, and cloud sync works again on the GitHub Pages version of the app.
  • Voice and Accessibility PolishVoice scoring handles themes, presets, and sign-in more reliably. The mic button moves out of the keypad's way, screen readers skip hidden panels, reduced-motion settings are respected, and startup is faster.
