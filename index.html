About Rook Score

Rook Score

Fast, friendly scorekeeping for your Rook table.

Version 2.1 By Mark Heinonen

Quick Start

  1. 1 Tap a teamTap "Us" or "Dem" to begin a round.
  2. 2 Choose the bidPick the bid amount and select who took points.
  3. 3 Enter the scoreTap the score field to slide up the in-app keypad. The Us and Dem score cards update before you submit.

Pro Mode adds live win probability during play. Turn it on in Settings.

Key Features

  • Fast, Intuitive Scoring
  • Round-by-Round History
  • Save and Resume Games
  • Cloud Sync
  • Team Stats
  • Custom Themes
  • Dark Mode
  • Live Win Probability
  • Table-Talk Penalty: Flag the team that engaged in table-talk (cheating) for the round. Configure the penalty in Settings — deduct either their bid amount or a fixed number of points.

What's New in v2.1

  • Cartoony Glass ThemeThe default app style now opens with the new cartoony 3-D glass treatment.
  • Bolder Game CardsTeam cards, round cards, score entry, history, and game-over views have stronger depth, glow, and motion.
  • Polished ControlsButtons, inputs, navigation, color pickers, and modal surfaces now share the same bouncy interaction language.
  • Statistics RefreshStatistics rows, chips, segmented controls, KPI cards, and entity details were restyled to match the new theme.
  • PWA Color UpdateTheme and background colors were updated so installed/mobile app chrome matches the new visual direction.
